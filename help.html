Before your family game

  • Choose Local / Hot-Seat and Quick Deploy for phone-controller games.
  • Use Phone setup on the computer to create a room and assign each phone to a human commander.
  • One fortification ends your turn. Cards, research, promotions, diplomacy and handoff dialogs still use the computer.
  • Remote computer multiplayer is a separate mode; it cannot currently share a game with these phone controllers.
